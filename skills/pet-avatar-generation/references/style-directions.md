# Style Direction Guidance

Use these only when the user asks for multiple style options but does not supply enough directions of their own. Prefer the user's reference images and stated preferences over this catalog.

Choose three to five directions that are materially different in rendering language, not merely different background colors.

- **Dreamlike portrait**: soft pastel palette, gentle glow, airy gradients or bokeh, painterly detail, expressive but faithful eyes.
- **Cute chibi**: simplified rounded shapes, enlarged head and eyes, compact proportions, soft shading, while preserving distinctive markings and ear/muzzle shape.
- **Minimal vector**: clean geometric silhouettes, flat or lightly shaded color fields, crisp edges, strong readability at small sizes.
- **Storybook watercolor**: visible paper or wash texture, loose edges, warm hand-painted character, restrained detail.
- **Soft 3D toy**: plush, clay, or collectible-figure rendering with tactile material cues and studio-like lighting.
- **Graphic ink**: bold contour work, selective hatching or brush texture, limited palette, high-contrast silhouette.

Keep avatar composition broadly comparable across the exploration set so style—not framing or pet identity—drives the user's choice.
